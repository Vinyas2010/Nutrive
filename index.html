Dates

Dates contain a lot of energy, dietary fiber, and minerals[cite: 1]. That makes them an excellent healthy basis for any meal[cite: 1]. These fruits are good for the cardiovascular system due to the high potassium content, they help to restore the strength of the muscular system because of magnesium, and they also contain powerful antioxidants that protect the body from free radicals[cite: 1]. Dates are sweet enough even without any added sugar[cite: 1].
